House Render

A 3D render of my house

I recently bought a 1940s house that came with a list of issues outlined in the homebuyer's report. I found the 80-page report incredibly difficult to digest. Sections also lacked visual context so I had to keep going back and forth to understand what they were referring to.

I built this 3D tool to map out the problem areas and track what we've fixed. The house is rendered in 3D so I can visually see where the problems lie. The problems, marked 1 to 3 with 3 being severe, are colour-coded to highlight the areas needing the most attention. When I click on an area of the house the problems are shown in the side menu with the exact wording from the buyer's report.

The tool also acts as a general to-do list. I can add my own issues like restoring the door frames. It works on mobile so I'm able to take pictures of the tasks I add. I added Claude's API so it can analyse the task and any attached imagery and offer a step-by-step guide including warnings such as lead paint.

Whilst I pretty much doxxed myself by posting this publicly on LinkedIn and Twitter, I knew the site needed to be secure and only accessible to me. Login uses my email with verification codes. I can invite anyone else who may need it.
